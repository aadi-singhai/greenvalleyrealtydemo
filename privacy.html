Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This website, its enquiry form and the related Facebook lead form demonstrate a lead follow-up service. Green Valley Realty is a fictional business, and the projects shown are sample data.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), the project you are interested in, your budget, preferred location, buying timeline, and whether you agree to be contacted on WhatsApp. If you message us on WhatsApp, we also keep those messages.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your property enquiry: an automated WhatsApp assistant confirms your enquiry and answers general questions, and a property advisor contacts you by phone or WhatsApp.

Consent

We send WhatsApp messages only if you agree to it on the form, or if you message us first. Without consent, an advisor may call you about the enquiry you submitted, but you will not receive automated messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time. Automated messages stop immediately and we record your choice.

Who can see your data

Our property advisors and the service provider that operates the automation. Messages are delivered through WhatsApp (Meta) and assistant replies are generated by an AI provider that receives only the text needed to reply. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after your last enquiry, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by replying on WhatsApp or submitting the enquiry form with your request.
